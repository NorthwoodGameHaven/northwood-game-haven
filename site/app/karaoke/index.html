How it works

  1. Scan the QR on the TV (or type the code), pick your room, add your name.
  2. Add songs — up to 2 singers per song. Rooms take turns, round-robin.
  3. Sing — the performing room sings on the Haven's wireless mics, so the whole building hears the song; the mics in your own room are for cheering and commentary. Lyrics run on the TVs and on your phone.
  4. Score — one room performs, and only the other rooms rate it: song choice (up to 5 pts) and delivery (average stars × 2, up to 10 pts). Your room can't rate itself.
  5. Win — the room with the most points when the host calls it takes the crown.
