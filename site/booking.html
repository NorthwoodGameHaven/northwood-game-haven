Book a Play Space

Reserve The Holt, Stash's Den, or The Depths by the hour. Submit a request below — a Game Guru will review it and confirm by email. Payment is arranged after approval.

💳 A refundable deposit is required, and is calculated for each booking based on the room(s) you reserve. It's a hold, not an added fee — refunded in full so long as nothing is stolen or damaged and the room(s) are left reasonably cleaned. There is a 4-hour minimum on all play-space bookings. Your exact deposit and total are itemized in the cost summary at the bottom of this form. Full details in our Terms & Conditions.
