Stash's Treasure Hunt — Official Rules

Northwood Game Haven • 115 W Spring St, Chippewa Falls, WI 54729 • Last updated September 1, 2026

1. No purchase necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win. A purchase will not increase your chances of winning.

2. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Employees and Gurus of Northwood Game Haven and Northwood Experiences LLC, and members of their immediate households, are not eligible. Void where prohibited.

3. Promotion period

Entries are accepted from Tuesday, September 1, 2026 through Monday, September 7, 2026 (Labor Day) at 11:59 PM Central. Referred followers must follow and comment within the promotion period to count.

4. How to enter

  1. Like the official giveaway post on the Northwood Game Haven Facebook page or Instagram account.
  2. A friend who does not already follow Northwood Game Haven follows the page/account and comments on the giveaway post naming the person who sent them (the “Referrer”).
  3. Each verified new follower earns one Mystery Treasure entry for the Referrer and one for the new follower.

Tagging or sharing is welcome but not required. Accounts that already followed Northwood Game Haven before the promotion period, duplicate accounts, and accounts that unfollow before the drawing will not be counted. Northwood Game Haven determines, in its sole discretion, whether a follower is new and which Referrer they are credited to.

5. Prizes and number of winners

  • Stash's Mystery Treasure — five (5) winners. One entry per verified new follower. Each Mystery Treasure includes: 1× Allplay Lightweight Game Bag, 1× Board or Card Game, 1× Stash's Dice Set, 2× Pokémon Grab Bags, and 3× YanYan Snacks. Specific game, dice, and grab-bag contents are selected by Northwood Game Haven; no substitutions.
  • Stash's Bonus Treasure — three (3) winners, drawn from Referrers with three (3) or more verified new followers. Prize: one 4-hour private room rental at Northwood Game Haven, room of the winner's choice, subject to availability. Does not include the Mystery Treasure.
  • Stash's Grand Treasure — one (1) winner, drawn from Referrers with five (5) or more verified new followers. Prize: one complimentary stay at The Lodge or The Adventurer's Rest (Northwood Experiences LLC), winner's choice of property, as follows: one (1) night if the stay is booked for a Friday or Saturday night, or two (2) consecutive nights if the stay does not include a Friday or Saturday night. Subject to availability. Standard occupancy limits and lodging terms apply. Does not include the Mystery Treasure. Winner must be 21 or older to redeem the stay.

Entries are cumulative. A Referrer with 5+ verified new followers receives one (1) Grand Treasure entry, one (1) Bonus Treasure entry, and one (1) Mystery Treasure entry per verified new follower. A Referrer with 3–4 verified new followers receives one (1) Bonus Treasure entry plus one (1) Mystery Treasure entry per verified new follower. Each tier is drawn separately; qualifying for a higher tier never removes you from a lower-tier drawing. No person may win more than one prize per tier. Prizes have no cash value and may not be transferred, exchanged, or redeemed for cash. The Bonus Treasure room rental and the Grand Treasure stay must each be booked and completed within 6 months of winner notification.

6. Winner selection and notification

Winners will be selected by random drawing on Tuesday, September 8, 2026, the day after Labor Day, from all eligible entries. Winners will be announced on the Northwood Game Haven Facebook page and notified by direct message. A winner who does not respond within 7 days, or who is found ineligible, forfeits the prize and an alternate may be drawn. Mystery Treasure prizes must be picked up in store at 115 W Spring St, Chippewa Falls, WI.
