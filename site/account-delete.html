What gets deleted

  • Your customer record — name, email address and phone number
  • Your rewards balance and customer code
  • Room bookings and event registrations held under that address
  • Display names you have used in Karaoke Battle, Team Trivia and Speed Gaming

What we have to keep

Records of completed purchases — the receipt, what was bought and what it cost. We are required to keep those for tax and accounting, so they stay, with your personal details removed from them where we can. This is the same exception described in our privacy policy.

How long it takes

Within 30 days. In practice it is usually the next time somebody is at the desk.
