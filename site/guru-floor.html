How it's used

Table numbers must match pairing table numbers (default 1, 2, 3… or your custom table labels in game settings). On tournament TVs, the current round's players appear on their table and unfinished matches glow. The Studio's 🗺️ Floor-map slide shows this layout on ambient TVs too.

Canvas is the room at 1920×1080 (shown half-size). Drag to place; seats draw automatically around each table.
