Privacy Policy

Northwood Game Haven · Updated 12 September 2026

This covers gamehaven.guru and the Game Haven mobile app. Both are run by Northwood Game Haven, 115 W Spring St, Chippewa Falls, WI 54729.

Who "we" means

Northwood Game Haven is ECCentric LLC, trading as Northwood Game Haven. It runs the café, the shop, the event rooms and your rewards account, and everything on this page describes what it does with your information. It is who to contact about any of it.

The Game Haven app is published on Google Play by Northwood Experiences LLC — a separate company in the same building, which also operates the two overnight suites. It publishes the app on Northwood Game Haven's behalf and does not receive the information described below.

Booking an overnight stay happens on VRBO, not here. The suite buttons in the app and on the website hand you over to VRBO's own site, where their privacy policy applies and we never see what you enter.

The short version. We collect what we need to hold your booking, get you into an event, and run your rewards account — nothing else. We don't sell your information, we don't run ads, and there are no advertising or analytics trackers in the app.

Camera

The app asks for the camera only to scan QR codes — joining a karaoke session or redeeming a special. Images are processed on your device as you point it at the code. Nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted, and every screen that uses the scanner also accepts a typed code, so you can decline the permission and still use the app.

What we do not collect

  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone recordings or health data.
  • No card numbers — those go straight to Stripe.

Who else is involved

We use a small number of services to run the business. They only receive what they need to do their job, and none of them receive your information for their own marketing.

  • Stripe — card payments and refunds.
  • Lightspeed — point of sale, purchase history and the rewards programme.
  • Netlify and Neon — website hosting and the database behind bookings and events.
  • Email delivery — booking confirmations, tickets and event notices.

We do not sell or rent your information to anyone, for any purpose.

How long we keep it

Booking and order records are kept while they're needed to run and account for the business. Event display names — team names, karaoke names, Speed Gaming check-ins — are part of that event and are cleared when the event ends. Rewards history lives in your Lightspeed account for as long as that account exists.

Children

The Game Haven app is a general-audience app for a family venue. It isn't directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Younger guests are welcome in the shop — a parent or guardian books and registers on their behalf. If you believe a child has given us information directly, email us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • See or correct what we hold. Email us and we'll tell you what we have and fix anything wrong.
  • Delete it. Ask and we'll delete your details, other than records we're required to keep for tax and accounting. Use gamehaven.guru/account-delete, or in the app go to Rewards → Delete my account. You don't need the app installed and you don't need to be signed in. A person here handles it within 30 days and emails you when it's done.
  • Rewards. Ask any Guru to close your rewards account.
  • Emails. Every non-transactional email has an unsubscribe link. Booking confirmations and tickets aren't marketing and will still be sent.
  • Camera. Decline it, or turn it off later in your phone's app settings. The scanner stops working; everything else carries on.
  • App data. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored on your device.

Security

The site and the app talk to our servers over HTTPS. Staff access to the Guru console is password-protected. Card details never touch our systems. No system is perfect, and we won't pretend otherwise — but we keep the amount we hold small on purpose, which is the most reliable protection there is.

Changes

If this changes we'll update the date at the top of the page. Anything significant, we'll say so in the app.
