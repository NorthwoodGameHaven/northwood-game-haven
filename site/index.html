Welcome to the Haven

The Chippewa Valley's First of Its Kind

Northwood Game Haven is more than a game store. We're a full-service gathering place where complete newcomers and lifelong hobbyists sit at the same table. Our Game Gurus — passionate, category-expert players — will teach, set up, and guide you through any game in our library. Our exclusive Journey system gently guides first-timers into the hobby. And when the night runs long, you can stay.


Thousands of games. Private rooms with real character. A retro video game library spanning a dozen classic consoles. The Chippewa Valley's largest craft soda selection. And a multi-level lounge deck overlooking the river and downtown Chippewa Falls — all under one roof at 115 W Spring St.


Same great food. New interactive atmosphere. We've partnered with beloved local restaurants to bring full menu items right to your table, private room, or overnight suite — no need to leave the game. Order in, stay in the zone. And if you're planning a special event away from the Haven, our Game Catering Experience brings a dedicated Game Guru — and a curated game library — right to you.

Game Library & Café

Teach & Play

Never played a board game before? No problem. Our staff will sit down and teach you any game in our library — no rulebook required, no experience needed. Just show up curious and we'll handle the rest. Gaming is for everyone, and we mean it.

Perfect for First-Timers

The Journey System

Completely new to the hobby? Our retail floor is built around our exclusive "Journey" system — a guided path that introduces newcomers to modern board gaming with no overwhelm and no jargon. Just discovery, one great game at a time.

Exclusive to Northwood Game Haven

Retail Shop

Board games, trading card games (MTG, Pokémon, Riftbound & more), accessories, and local goods. New and used inventory. Whether you're a veteran collector or buying your very first game, we'll help you find exactly the right fit.

Retro Video Game Library

A dozen classic consoles — NES through GameCube and beyond — stocked with the best games for each platform. Check out any system with any room reservation. Private Room
The Depths

The ultimate D&D lair. Antique glass cabinetry with RGB ambient lighting, authentic CRT TV gaming across 3 premium vintage CRT TVs, and a bar to keep the adventure going. The most immersive room in the house — and like all our rooms, equipped with retro console hook-ups.

The crown jewel of The Depths is the Game Theory "D&D Table of Adventure" — a one-of-a-kind masterpiece exclusively designed in collaboration with Wizards of the Coast. Loaded with all the bells and whistles: accessory trays, dice vaults, GM screen mounts, and premium accessories that elevate every session to legendary status.
